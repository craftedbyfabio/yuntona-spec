# Draft runs 5 and 6, 27 November 2026

Grading instructions with cross-references followed (§5) and the Proposed-by wording corrected,
signal register 2 (draft), suite 1 (52 cases; 51 reach a grader, 1 is dropped by code). Both graders
were Claude models (a mid-size and a large one), so this checks the instructions and the tests, not
independence between model families. Pipeline checks (`tools/test_pipeline.py`) pass.

## What the runs found

- **Run 5: 51 of 52.** IN-04 failed: one grader read "Control what your agents can do" as the
  vendor's claim on ASI02. The cause was the instructions, which said that a missing Proposed-by line
  meant the vendor itself had mapped the evidence. Corrected: the vendor's mapping is only what the
  evidence shows (a sentence naming the risk, or a vendor heading for it).
- **Run 5 also exposed a pipeline bug.** One grader wrote empty fields as `""` and the other as
  `null`, and the comparison treated them as a disagreement on every row. Fixed, with a check.
- **Run 6: 51 of 52.** CS-01 failed: its evidence named no risk and had no vendor heading, so under
  the corrected wording nobody had mapped it and one grader answered *no evidence found*, which the
  rules support. The case was written before that rule; it now sits under the vendor's ASI03 heading.
  Both graders regraded CS-01 alone and answered *claimed only*.
